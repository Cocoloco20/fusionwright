## Notes

- Front doors: the framed panel under the scoop in the 3/4 view is a pair of doors with two hinges per door on the outer edges, push-to-open so nothing shows on the front. With door_span='full' (default) they span the whole front between a fixed corner slat on each side, onto one big bay holding a single wide pull-out drawer on 18 in full-extension slides; shelves over the bay roof the tower and step compartments above it, which still open through their lids. door_span='wide' limits it to the centre and step sections, 'centre' to the centre section; door_leaves=1 gives a single door, door=False none, drawer=False an empty bay; door_open_deg=90 and drawer_out=12 show it open.
- Read from the sketch: arc-cut slat tops, five lids at 24/19/14 in, stepped boxes, L-shaped corner feet hiding four casters (plate screwed under a top plate), slats stopping above the floor on an apron.
- Interpretations to confirm with the designer: the dark squares (tower fronts) and dark wedges (step fronts) are modelled as finger openings through the plywood front wall; the tilted blocks in the 3/4 view are the lids shown open (use open_deg); the scoop is cut in both the front and the back skin (arc_on_back).
- My proposal, not in the sketch: plywood carcass, dividers, lid hinges at the back, centre-lid finger notch, apron height, caster size.
- Slats are butt-fastened to the plywood carcass; use glue plus brad nails or screws from the inside.
